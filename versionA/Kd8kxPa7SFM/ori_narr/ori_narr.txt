 Their two of the greatest artists of the 16th century, Lukas Khanach, known as the Elder,  and his son, also named Lukas Khanach.  They ran the most prolific workshop of their time, painting more and faster than any  of their contemporaries.  The Protestant Reformer, Martin Luther, was a close friend, and the Khanach, became the  painters of the Reformation.  Born at the end of the Middle Ages, the artists lived and made their fortune in a world  that was rapidly changing.  Both father and son embody ideas associated with the term Renaissance, a time of revival,  and a pival in art.
